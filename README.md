# Examining an Intrusion Detection Security Policy

## Objective of the Lab 
In this lab, you will learn to examine an intrusion detection security policy. This policy establishes intrusion detection and security monitoring to protect resources and data on the organizational network. 
## Instructions 
### STEP 1
From the desktop, open Firefox. 
### STEP 2
In the address bar, enter the url as https://www.sans.org/security-resources/policies 
### Caution
At the View Cookie Policy pop-up, click Accept All Cookies and Close. 
### STEP 3
In the Search box, type (disaster), and from the result list, select The Disaster Recovery Plan.
### STEP 4
On the The Disaster Recovery Plan page, click Download.
### SEP 5
Review the policy pdf.
### STEP 6 
In the upper-right corner of the PDF, click Download to download the PDF. 
### STEP 7
Close all windows.
